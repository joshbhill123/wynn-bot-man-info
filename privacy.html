Privacy policy

Wynn Bot-man is a personal application operated by Joshua Hill.

Google data access and use

The application requests Gmail read-only access to find competition correspondence and download fixture and standings workbooks. Its separate sending credential sends completed tip workbooks and necessary project correspondence from the owner's Gmail account.

Google permissions apply to the mailbox, but the application's intended use is limited to running this football tipping project. Mail content, attachments, predictions and delivery receipts may be processed by the owner's authorised automation and AI tools for that purpose.

Storage and sharing

Downloaded competition messages, attachments and working records are stored on the owner's computer. OAuth credentials are stored outside the source repository. Checked submissions are shared with the competition organiser. Competition and mailbox data are not published on this information site, sold, or used for advertising.

Google provides Gmail and authentication. GitHub Pages hosts this public information site and may process ordinary technical request information under GitHub's privacy statement. This site includes no analytics scripts, advertising or sign-in forms.

Retention and control

Competition records are retained for running and checking the competition. The owner can remove locally stored records and revoke the application's Google access in Google Account settings. Revoking access does not delete records already downloaded or emails already sent.

Wynn Bot-man's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
