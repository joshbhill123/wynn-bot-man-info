Wynn Bot-man

Wynn Bot-man is Joshua Hill's private football tipping assistant. It reads competition emails and spreadsheet attachments from the competition organiser, prepares predictions and a completed workbook, and emails the checked submission back to that organiser.

This application is for its owner's personal use. It has no public registration or hosted mailbox service. The public site contains application information only.

Google access uses separate Gmail read-only and send permissions. The application runs locally on the owner's computer.
